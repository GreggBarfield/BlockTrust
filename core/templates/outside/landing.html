Overview

The cattle supply chain is complex. It is one of the few large industries in the world with multiple sectors that each produce critical production data that they are still unable to share. The hard workers in each sector have the skills and knowledge necessary to create a great product, but often do not have enough definitive data to establish many of the precise benchmarks necessary for improving practices and making good buying decisions.

This information void is not because of the lack of software or the reluctance to use it; there is very good software being used in each sector that produces a wealth of critical production data. The problem is that the data remains in that sector even though it is important for other sectors to use.

An example of this is carcass data. Carcass data is the result of the grading process in packing facilities and is the largest deciding factor in determining the value of the end product. It is the goal of each sector to do their part to make an end-product that grades high but once the animal leaves their possession, they usually have no idea how it actually graded.

This is about to change.

Industry Spotlight: Our Auction Markets

Our nation's livestock auctions provide many producers a reliable and competitive place to market their livestock. These markets are a key part of the cattle industry and are in fact; a key part of many communities as noted in this Recent Article in Drovers.

Through the BlockTrust Network, auction houses now have access to critical production data on the animals going through their markets and are able to provide buyers more information to make quality purchasing decisions.
