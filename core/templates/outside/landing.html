Overview

The beef supply chain is complex. It is one of the few large industries in the world with multiple sectors that each produce critical production data that they are still unable to share. Cow-calf ranchers have the skills and knowledge necessary to create a great product, but often do not have enough definitive data to establish many of the precise benchmarks necessary for improving practices. Feeders and other cattle buyers are now demanding verifiable individual data on animals to help them make purchasing decisions.

Consumers are demanding to know more about the food they are eating. Ranchers need to tell the world about their contributions to animal welfare, carbon reduction, and sustainability efforts. The fragmented nature of the beef supply chain makes it impossible to tell that story.

There is very good software being used in each sector that produces a wealth of critical production data. The problem is that the data remains in that sector even though it is important for others to use.

This is about to change.

We Don't Track Anything. You Do.

We bring together everything that’s required to connect to your partners in the beef supply chain. Use our API’s and other tools to get carcass data, share animal metrics, and provide information to the consumer.

This is YOUR supply chain. Own it.

Security

Part of our security measures include using Blockchain technology. Blockchain is a system of recording information in a way that makes it difficult to change, hack, or cheat the system.

Privacy

No personal data goes into the system. You have complete control over what you want to share by setting your permissions on the portal.

Connectivity

Through our ever-growing list of network partners, you will be able to see data all the way up and down the supply chain, including carcass data.

Open Source

Your software provider connects to the network by using our open-source API's. We provide access to the software at no charge.
