RFID Tags

{% endblock outside_banner %} {% block outside_content %}

Cattle are traced by using RFID ear tags, which are scanned at each sector of the supply chain. You do not have to purchase tags from us to use the system, but we offer tags through this tag store for your convenience. You can also purchase scanners and various other items necessary for scanning. In order to purchase tags in the tag store, you have to have an account in the system. You can register for a FREE account here. If you do not buy tags from us, we provide a convenient way to upload both EID and visual tag numbers into the system so you can get data from upstream partners.
