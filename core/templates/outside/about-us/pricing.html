Pricing

As we transition out of the pilot projects into widespread industry adoption, we will only provide pricing models for associations and programs. An example of this would be a breed association, or a Processed-Verified Progarm (PVP). If you are part of an association or program and want to participate please contact us for information.

Future Models

There will never be a charge to upload data to our platform. You will only be charged if you access data from the system in the form of queries, reports, or downloads. There is no charge to access data if there are no matches with any other sectors on the network.

To access data, there will be several options to choose from. Monthly subscriptions give you access to an unlimited amount of data at any time. 'Per Transaction' fees give you access to your data whenever you need it and is charged on a per animal basis.

Private labeling and retail licenses are also available. Please contact us for information.
